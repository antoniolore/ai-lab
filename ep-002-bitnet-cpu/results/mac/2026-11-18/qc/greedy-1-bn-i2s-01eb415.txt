The history of the printing press begins in the 15th century, when Johannes Gutenberg, a German goldsmith, invented a machine that could mass-produce books. The printing press was a revolutionary invention that changed the way people read and write. It allowed for the mass production of books, making them more affordable and accessible to the general public. This led to a significant increase in literacy rates and the spread of knowledge.

The history of the printing press begins in the 15th century, when Johannes Gutenberg invented a machine that could mass-produce books. The printing press was a revolutionary invention that changed the way people communicated and shared information. It allowed for the mass production of books, which made them more affordable and accessible to the general public. This, in turn, led to a significant increase in literacy rates and the spread of knowledge.
